==Geography==
[[File:Moyemont - chemin bordé d'arbres - 2022.jpg|thumb|A tree-lined dirt track in Moyemont in summer]]
Moyemont is a rural commune positioned between [[Charmes, Vosges|Charmes]] to the west and [[Rambervillers]] some {{Convert|7|km|mi|0}} to the east.   To the south of the village, more than half of the commune's 1,230 hectares are covered by forest (''le forêt de Rambervillers'').   Two small streams, the Habu and the ForePrays ''(le devant Prays)'', both tributaries of the [[Mortagne (river)|Mortagne]] flow towards the north-east, while in the south of the commune other streams flow to the south and south-east, reflecting commune's modestly elevated topography.   The village itself is at the confluence of several small roads, with a lay-out in an irregular star shape resulting.   The least minor of the roads crossing the village is the Route Departmentale RD32.

==History==
[[File:Pilori Niort.jpg|thumb|left|upright=0.7|The Pilori (the Pillory) which served as the town hall before the French Revolution]]
The first town hall in Niort was at the corner of Rue du Rempart and Rue Ricard. The aldermen relocated to an early iteration of the Pilori (the Pillory) in 1370: this square-shaped building, designed in the [[Medieval architecture|medieval style]], was re-built by the master mason, Mathurin Berthomé, in 1535.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.niortmaraispoitevin.com/en/patrimoine-culturel/le-pilori-espace-darts-visuels/ |title= Le Pilori – Espace d'arts visuels|publisher= Niort Marais Poitevin Tourisme| access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url= https://monumentum.fr/monument-historique/pa00101287/niort-ancien-hotel-de-ville-dit-le-pilori|title= Former town hall, known as Le Pilori|publisher=Monumentum| access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref> After the [[French Revolution]], the council was based at the governor's house in the grounds of the [[Donjon de Niort]].<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.vivre-a-niort.com/services-publics/annuaire-des-services/coordonnees/hotel-de-ville/index.html |title=Hôtel de Ville|publisher=Vivre à Niort|access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref>

In the late 19th century, the town council decided to commission a new town hall. The site they selected was occupied by the old Collège de l'Oratoire (College of the [[Oratory of Jesus]]),<ref>{{cite news|url= https://www.ouest-france.fr/sciences/archeologie/ce-que-lon-sait-sur-lancien-oratoire-a-niort-apres-la-decouverte-de-vestiges-au-pied-de-la-mairie-d4f1178c-be56-11ed-9958-ce650855cbcb |title= Ce que l'on sait sur l'ancien oratoire à Niort, après la découverte de vestiges au pied de la mairie|date=9 March 2023|newspaper= Ouest France| access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url= https://www.francebleu.fr/infos/culture-loisirs/sous-la-place-de-la-mairie-de-niort-des-fouilles-revelent-les-vestiges-d-un-ancien-batiment-religieux-2957889 |title= Sous la place de la mairie de Niort, des fouilles révèlent les vestiges d'un ancien oratoire|date=16 March 2023|newspaper=France Bleu| access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref> which was established in Niort in 1617.<ref>{{cite book|url=https://ia601603.us.archive.org/32/items/bub_gb_Aqk-AAAAcAAJ/bub_gb_Aqk-AAAAcAAJ.pdf |title= L'oratoire et le collège di Niort: Notice historique|first=J.-A. |last=Houteiller |page=8|publisher=E. Aubert|year=1865}}</ref> The foundation stone for the new building was laid by the president of France, [[Félix Faure]], on 27 April 1897.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.niortmaraispoitevin.com/en/patrimoine-culturel/hotel-de-ville-de-niort/ |title= Hôtel de Ville de Niort|publisher= Niort Marais Poitevin Tourisme| access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref> It was designed by Georges Lasseron in the [[Renaissance Revival architecture|Renaissance Revival style]], built in [[ashlar]] stone and was officially opened by the mayor, Ludovic Martin-Bastard, and the minister of justice, [[Ernest Monis]], over several days between 25 and 28 May 1901.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.vivre-a-niort.com/cadre-de-vie/decouvrir-niort/patrimoine-architectural/xxe-et-xxie-siecles/index.html |title=XXe et XXIe siecles|publisher=Vivre à Niort|access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.pss-archi.eu/immeubles/FR-79191-84863.html |title= Hôtel de ville de Niort|publisher=PSS Architecture| access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://structurae.net/fr/ouvrages/hotel-de-ville-de-niort|title=Hôtel de ville de Niort|publisher=Structurae| access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref>

The design involved a symmetrical main frontage of 11 bays facing onto the market square (now known as Place Martin-Bastard) with the end bays projected forward as [[pavilion]]s. The central bay featured a round headed opening with a moulded surround and a [[Keystone (architecture)|keystone]]. The other bays on the ground floor in the central section were fenestrated by round headed windows, while the bays on the first floor were fenestrated with square headed windows. The end bays were fenestrated in a similar style and were surmounted by [[pyramid]]-shaped roofs with [[dormer]] windows. There were fine carvings between each of the bays on the first floor of the central section and there was a clock, supported by ornate carvings and surmounted by a [[coat of arms]], above the central three bays. A tall [[octagon]]al [[belfry]] was erected behind the clock. Internally the principal rooms are the Salle du Conseil (council chamber) and the Salle de Mariages (wedding room). The paintings for these rooms were created by Charles Fouqueray.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.proantic.com/en/725692-charles-fouqueray-1869-1956-quotsea-salvagequot-1939.html |title=Charles Fouqueray (1869–1956): "Sea Salvage" |year=1939|publisher=Proantic| access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.francebleu.fr/emissions/histoires-du-poitou-avec-patrick-sitaud/poitou/pourquoi-y-a-t-il-un-lien-entre-l-hotel-de-ville-de-niort-et-le-peintre-charles-de-fouqueray |title=Pourquoi y a t-il un lien entre l'hôtel de ville de Niort et le peintre Charles de Fouqueray?|date=25 May 2018|newspaper=France Bleu|access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref>

[[File:Niort - hôtel de ville - 2026.jpg|thumb|upright|The façade and front steps of the town hall]]
Following the liberation of the town on 29 August 1944, during the [[World War II|Second World War]], a crowd of some 10,000 people assembled outside the town hall to celebrate on 6 September 1944. A member of the [[French Resistance]], Émile Bèche, was proclaimed mayor on the steps of the town hall.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.vivre-a-niort.com/actualites/dernieres-infos/il-y-a-75-ans-niort-liberee-8814/index.html |title=75 years ago: Niort, liberated!|publisher=Vivre à Niort|access-date=5 January 2025}}</ref>

In the early 21st century, two curved annexes were built to the east of the town hall to accommodate the demands for space for extra administrative staff: these buildings were designed by Jean-François Milou, and completed in March 2000.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.studiomilouporter.com.au/projects/hotel-administratif-niort/ |title= Hôtel Administratif, Niort, France|publisher=Studio Milou & Porter| access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref> In August 2023, an extensive programme of engineering works, involving the pedestrianisation of the area around the town hall, got underway.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.ouest-france.fr/nouvelle-aquitaine/niort-79000/niort-le-nouveau-visage-du-quartier-de-l-hotel-de-ville-se-dessine-acee3b80-5cb8-11ec-9016-85f0948c968a|title=Niort. Le nouveau visage du quartier de l’hôtel de ville se dessine|date=14 December 2021| newspaper=Ouest France| access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.ouest-france.fr/societe/urbanisme/a-niort-le-nouveau-visage-de-la-rue-de-lhotel-de-ville-en-2024-c4801bce-432a-11ee-ac21-50173a5f08fe |title=À Niort, le nouveau visage de la rue de l'Hôtel-de-Ville en 2024|date=29 August 2023|newspaper=Ouest France| access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://actu.fr/nouvelle-aquitaine/niort_79191/niort-les-travaux-rue-de-l-hotel-de-ville-debutent-ce-lundi_59988689.html|title=Niort. Les travaux rue de l'Hôtel de ville débutent ce lundi|date=21 August 2023|newspaper=Actu|access-date=20 December 2024}}</ref>

=== Reconversión nos 2000 e actualidade ===
[[Ficheiro:6 grúas torre en la Rehabilitación de la Universidad Laboral. Dirección de Obra. Sergio J. Barragán Arévalo.jpg|miniatura|350px|Rehabilitación en febreiro de 2006]]
En 2001, o [[Ministerio de Educación, Formación Profesional e Deportes (España)|Ministerio de Educación]] cedeu a titularidade do complexo ao [[Consello de Goberno do Principado de Asturias|Goberno do Principado de Asturias]].<ref name=":5">{{Cita publicación|url=https://oa.upm.es/51447/|título=Uso y cambio de uso en la Universidad Laboral de Gijón|apelidos=Vallina-Victorero Sánchez|nome=Lucía|data=2018|publicación=E.T.S. Arquitectura (UPM)}}</ref> 

En maio de 2003 presentouse o Plan de Usos no que se detallan as funcións que tería cada sector da edificación.<ref name=":5" /> Para habilitar estes usos realizáronse ata 18 adecuacións e rehabilitacións diferentes do edificio ao longo da década. Foron coordinadas por Antonio E. Cuartas e Sergio Barragán, baixo a supervisión do Ministerio de Educación e Cultura.<ref name=":5" />​ O groso das reformas durarían entre 2005 e 2007<ref name=":6">{{Cita web|url=http://www.laboralciudaddelacultura.com/es/1/ciudad-de-la-cultura/2/historia.html|título=Laboral Ciudad de la Cultura: Transformación|data-acceso=2022-09-22|páxina-web=www.laboralciudaddelacultura.com|data-arquivo=07 de xuño de 2023|url-arquivo=https://web.archive.org/web/20230607064714/http://www.laboralciudaddelacultura.com/es/1/ciudad-de-la-cultura/2/historia.html|url-morta=yes}}</ref>​ e concluíron na súa totalidade en 2008. Só un elemento foi quedou sen concluír por mor da crise: un hotel de cinco estrelas.<ref name=":5" />

As actuacións foron [[licitación|licitadas]] entre 2004 e 2007 por 77,8 millóns de euros, aínda que o orzamento definitivo de execución alcanzou os 82,2 millóns de euros. O maior sobrecusto foi a reforma do teatro, que pasou de 7,7 millóns a 11,57 millóns de euros.<ref>{{Cita novas|título=El Principado admite un 5,5% de desfase de gasto en la reforma de integral de la Laboral|url=https://hemeroteca.elcomercio.es/18/06/2008/18/2dc4030966e98d126a90b9ba1c3fad2d.html?subedition=AVI|data=18 de xuño de 2008|xornal=La Voz de Avilés}}</ref>

A renovación das instalacións incluiríase baixo a marca ''Laboral, ciudad de la cultura''.<ref name=":6" /> A “Cidade da Cultura” sería a principal aposta cultural do goberno de [[Vicente Álvarez Areces]] e sería inaugurada formalmente por este o [[29 de marzo]] de [[2007]].<ref>{{Cita novas|título=Laboral Ciudad de la Cultura ya es una realidad abierta al público|url=https://hemeroteca.elcomercio.es/30/03/2007/70/ddc7c19243b1fd3c486e01d2a38b0ae0.html?subedition=GIJ|data=30 de marzo de 2007|xornal=El Comercio|páxinas=70}}</ref><ref name=":6" />

En 2016 o edificio e a súa contorna foron declarados [[Ben de Interese Cultural]].<ref>{{Cita web|url=https://www.lavozdeasturias.es/noticia/gijon/2016/07/12/universidad-laboral-gijon-declarada-bien-interes-cultural/00031468318406500592645.htm|título=La Universidad Laboral de Gijón, declarada Bien de Interés Cultural|data-acceso=24 de marzo de 2024|data=12 de xullo de 2016|páxina-web=La Voz de Asturias}}</ref>

[[Ficheiro:Gijón - Universidad Laboral, tour et église - 2026.jpg|miniatura|upright|A torre e a igrexa da Universidade Laboral en 2026]]

Custaron algo menos de 800&nbsp;000 e remataron en xuño de 2024.<ref>{{Cita web|url=https://www.lne.es/gijon/2023/07/05/rehabilitacion-cupula-laboral-comienza-seis-89479095.html|título=La rehabilitación en la cúpula de la Laboral comienza tras seis años de fiascos|data-acceso=24 de marzo de 2024|apelidos=Cuesta|nome=Gabriel|data=5 de xullo de 2023|páxina-web=La Nueva España}}</ref><ref>{{Cita web|url=https://www.elcomercio.es/gijon/obra-cupula-laboral-retrasa-junio-restaurar-cruces-20240312231746-nt.html|título=La obra de la cúpula de la Laboral se retrasa a junio para restaurar las cruces|data-acceso=24 de marzo de 2024|apelidos=Moro|nome=Marcos|data=12 de marzo de 2024|páxina-web=El Comercio: Diario de Asturias}}</ref>
